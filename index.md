## 🧠 Understanding Context (In Simple Terms)

When you use AI coding tools, they read your project files to understand what you’re working on. This is called "context." If your project is messy, the AI has to read more files, which makes it slower and less accurate.

harness-audit reduces the amount of unnecessary context by cleaning up your project. This means:
- Your AI responds faster
- Your AI gives better answers
- Your projects stay manageable, even as they grow

The tool shows you exactly how much context you saved, so you can see the value of keeping things organized.
